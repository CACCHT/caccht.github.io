Our preferred practice is for developers to let users obtain datasets directly from the official repositories and import or load them through a separate, user-initiated process. We encourage developers to keep the datasets separate from paid products and subscriptions, preserving their independent availability and provenance. These are recommendations, not additional license conditions; compliant redistribution remains permitted.

Please cite the dataset and version used. This statement summarizes the license without adding conditions. The full license governs, and uses that do not require permission under applicable law remain unaffected.
